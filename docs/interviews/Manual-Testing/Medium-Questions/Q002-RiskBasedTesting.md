# Q002 - What is Risk-Based Testing?

Risk-based testing focuses testing efforts on areas with the highest risk of failure or business impact. It helps prioritize effort where defects would be most costly.

## Key Points
- Prioritizes critical features and high-risk modules.
- Reduces time and cost by focusing on important areas.
- Useful in time-constrained projects.

## Example Answer
Risk-based testing is a testing approach where the team identifies potential risks in the application and gives more attention to features that are more critical or more likely to fail. This ensures important business areas are tested first.
