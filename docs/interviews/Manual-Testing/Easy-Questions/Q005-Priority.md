# Q005 - What is Priority?

Priority describes how quickly a defect should be fixed. It is related to business urgency and scheduling.

## Common Priority Levels
- High
- Medium
- Low

## Example Answer
Priority tells the team how urgently a bug needs attention. For example, if a login issue affects a major release, it may be given high priority even if the technical severity is moderate. Priority is about business impact and urgency.
