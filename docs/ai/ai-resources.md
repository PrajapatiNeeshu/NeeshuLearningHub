# AI Resources & Stanford Curriculum

## Stanford's Free Online AI Curriculum

Learn core AI concepts from Stanford professors. Here are the top 7 essential courses:

1. **Artificial Intelligence – CS221**
   - Learn core AI concepts: search, logic, planning, decision-making
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gM892TxC)

2. **Machine Learning – CS229**
   - Learn supervised, unsupervised learning + algorithm design
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gWgtbwZc)

3. **Deep Learning – CS230**
   - Master neural nets, CNNs, RNNs, and best practices
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gYyD2Fwa)

4. **Machine Learning Theory – CS229M**
   - Explore the math behind learning systems and models
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gN4v2u9w)

5. **Reinforcement Learning – CS234**
   - Explore Q-learning, MDPs, and training models to take actions
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gKhtZYsV)

6. **Natural Language Understanding – CS224U**
   - Understand meaning, intent, and dialogue systems
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gg2BAW9k)

7. **NLP with Deep Learning – CS224N**
   - Learn how neural networks power translation, Q&A, and sentiment tasks.
   - [Course Link](https://lnkd.in/gWvnNXB2)

*Use these courses to go from "using AI" to actually building it.*
